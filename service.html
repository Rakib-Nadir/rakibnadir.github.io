Active Directory Security Assessment

ATTACK PATHS · KERBEROS · PERMISSIONS

Request Scoping

Active Directory is how most companies get fully compromised. I map the paths an attacker could take from a normal user account to Domain Admin, then show you which settings and permissions to fix to close them.

  • Kerberoasting and AS-REP roasting
  • BloodHound attack path mapping
  • Group Policy and delegation review
  • Domain hardening recommendations

How It Works

  1. 1

    Discovery & Scoping

    We agree on what gets tested, the rules of engagement and the timeline. You sign a written authorization before any testing starts.

  2. 2

    Testing

    Automated scanning plus manual testing. Critical issues are reported to you right away, not held for the final report.

  3. 3

    Report

    A clear report with severity ratings (CVSS v3.1), proof for every issue and how to fix it. I can walk your team through it on a call.

  4. 4

    Free Retest

    Once you fix the issues, I retest them at no extra cost and confirm they are closed.

What You Get

  • Executive Summary A short non-technical overview of the risks and what to fix first, written for management.
  • Technical Findings Steps, requests and screenshots so your developers can reproduce each issue.
  • Fix Guidance Practical fix instructions and code examples for each finding, plus a retest letter once issues are closed.
